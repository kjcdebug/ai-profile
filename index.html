My AI Profile

One prompt. AI interviews you, then writes your settings. About 15 minutes.

  1. Copy the prompt with the button below.
  2. Paste it into a new chat in your company ChatGPT account and answer its questions.
  3. Save what it writes: ChatGPT Settings > Personalization > Custom instructions.
Company work goes in your company ChatGPT account. Never a personal one.
I want you to write custom instructions for my AI settings, so you know who I am and how to work with me every time we talk.

First, interview me.
- Ask me 10 to 15 questions in total, in 3 rounds of about 5. Wait for my answers before starting the next round.
- Use multiple choice whenever you can. Number each question, letter each option (A, B, C...), and always include "Other: ____" as the last option, so I can answer like "1B, 2A, 3 Other: service calls." Use a short-answer question only when multiple choice won't work.
- Cover: my role and what I'm responsible for day to day; who and what I manage (people, crews, jobs, equipment, budgets); the tasks that take up most of my time; what I'd most like help with; how I like answers (length, format, tone, level of detail); trade terms you should know; what matters most to me at work; anything you should never do.

Rules for the whole conversation:
- Never assume anything about me. If something is unclear, or I skipped a question, ask me. I'd rather you ask every time than state a guess as fact.
- If you ever have to infer something, label it ASSUMPTION so I can check it.
- Don't ask for, or include, passwords, pay, personal ID numbers or customer details.

When the interview is done:
1. Show me a short summary of what you learned and ask me to correct anything wrong.
2. Once I confirm, write my custom instructions as one block of text in a single copy box, ready to paste into my settings. Start with who I am and what I do, then how I want you to respond.
3. Write it in first person, in plain language, under 1,500 characters.
4. Include this rule, word for word: "Do not make assumptions. If you are unsure or missing information, ask me. Label anything you inferred as an ASSUMPTION."

Start with round 1.
